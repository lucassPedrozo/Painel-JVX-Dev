# Product Overview

JVX Desenvolvimento is a comprehensive web development project management system designed for managing client projects, payments, and team productivity.

## Core Purpose
- Track web development projects from inception to delivery
- Monitor payment status and financial metrics
- Analyze developer productivity and project timelines
- Generate reports and export data for business insights

## Key Features
- **Project Management**: Full CRUD operations for development projects with status tracking
- **Payment Control**: Track payment status, pending amounts, and financial analytics
- **Team Management**: Developer profiles with contact and payment information
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Real-time statistics, charts, and productivity metrics
- **Multi-user System**: Role-based access (Master/Standard) with JWT authentication
- **Data Import/Export**: CSV import for bulk data entry and PDF export for reports
- **Calendar Integration**: Project delivery timeline visualization

## Target Users
- **Development Agencies**: Managing multiple client projects and developers
- **Freelance Teams**: Coordinating work and tracking payments
- **Project Managers**: Overseeing development timelines and deliverables

## Business Model
Internal management tool for development agencies to streamline project tracking, improve productivity analysis, and maintain financial oversight of ongoing projects.
